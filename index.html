About the studio

Beyond design. Into legacy.

Asshwini Ghodke Interiors & Associates is a premier interior design and architecture practice based in Indore. We create homes, commercial spaces, and landscapes that outlast trends and deepen with time.

Every commission begins with listening. We study how a family gathers, how light crosses a room at four in the afternoon, how a building should meet its landscape. From that understanding comes a clear idea, refined until nothing is left that does not belong.

Architectural philosophy

Architecture is the discipline of restraint. We shape volume, proportion and silence so that a building feels inevitable rather than imposed.

Signature approach

One team carries each project from first sketch to final styling, so architecture, interiors and landscape speak in a single voice.

Commitment to timeless design

We choose honest materials, precise craft and enduring geometry, designing for the next generation as much as the present one.

Luxury Residential Architecture

The Azure Residence

Location
Indore, Madhya Pradesh
Year
2025
Area
1,240 sq m

Set on a steep lakeside plot, the Azure Residence is organised as three stacked stone and glass volumes that step down toward the water. Each level opens to a terrace carved from the hillside, framing the lake like a living painting. Inside, travertine, smoked oak and bronze create a calm palette that lets the view lead.

Deep overhangs shade the interiors in summer, while a concealed courtyard pulls light into the heart of the plan. The result is a home that feels both monumental and quiet.

The design philosophy

Design is not just what we create. It is what we leave behind.

Timeless design

We design beyond fashion. Calm proportions and clear ideas keep a space relevant long after trends have moved on.

Material selection

Stone, timber and metal are chosen for how they age, and every surface is tested in real light before it is specified.

Spatial experience

We choreograph arrival, compression and release so each movement through a building has rhythm and purpose.

Light and geometry

Daylight is our primary material. Geometry directs it, softens it and gives each hour of the day its own character.

Functionality and aesthetics

Beauty must work. Storage, acoustics and comfort are resolved so the elegance never has to be compromised.

Our creative process

Five stages from idea to reveal.

  1. 01

    Discovery & Consultation

    We learn your goals, lifestyle, site and budget through in-depth conversations and site visits.

  2. 02

    Concept & Vision

    Sketches, mood studies and massing models express a clear design idea for your approval.

  3. 03

    Design Development

    Plans, materials, lighting and details are refined, with full visualisations and technical drawings.

  4. 04

    Execution & Coordination

    We coordinate contractors, craftspeople and suppliers, supervising quality on site at every milestone.

  5. 05

    Final Reveal

    Furniture, art and styling are placed, and the finished space is handed over ready to live in.
